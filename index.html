2. What we collect

We only ask for the information we need to help you and your car. Depending on how you contact us, this may include:

  • Your contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Vehicle details: your registration number, make and model, and anything else you tell us such as the year or mileage.
  • Enquiry details: the service you're interested in and any description of the problem you give us.
  • MOT bookings: the date and time you choose, plus any notes you add.
  • Basic technical data: when you visit the site, our hosting provider may automatically log things like your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you visit. This helps keep the site running and secure.

We don't ask for sensitive information (such as health details) through this website, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

3. Why we use it and our lawful basis

UK data protection law says we must have a valid reason, called a lawful basis, for using your personal information. Here's what we use it for and why:

What we use it forLawful basis
Replying to your enquiry and giving you a quoteTaking steps at your request before entering a contract, or our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us
Booking, confirming and carrying out your MOT, and contacting you about itContract (or steps before entering one)
Keeping MOT and work records required by the DVSA, and accounting and tax recordsLegal obligation
Running and improving our business, keeping customer records, dealing with questions or complaints, and keeping the website secureLegitimate interests

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced our interests against yours and only use your information in ways you would reasonably expect. We will not use your details for marketing unless you have asked us to, and you can tell us to stop at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We only share it where we need to, with:

  • MOTmanager: the booking and garage management system we use to manage MOT bookings and customer records.
  • The DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency): when we carry out an MOT, test results and vehicle details are recorded on the official MOT testing service, as the law requires.
  • Website hosting and email providers: the companies that host this website and handle our email, so that your messages and bookings reach us.
  • Google: our Find Us section uses an embedded Google Map. When it loads, Google may collect information such as your IP address and set cookies, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Others where required by law: for example the police, HMRC or our professional advisers, if we are legally required to or need to protect our legal rights.

The service providers we use must keep your information secure and only use it to provide their services to us. Some of them may store data outside the UK; where they do, we make sure suitable safeguards required by UK law are in place.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. This website itself does not set any tracking or advertising cookies, and we don't use analytics tools to follow you around the web.

However, the embedded Google Map on our homepage may set its own cookies or collect data when it loads. These are controlled by Google, not by us. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can still find us without the map: our address is Units A2 & A3 Crabtree Rd, Egham TW20 8RN.

6. How long we keep it

We only keep your information for as long as we need it. As a guide:

  • Enquiries that don't go any further: around 12 months, so we can pick up where we left off if you get back in touch.
  • Customer, MOT and work records: around 6 years, to meet legal, accounting and DVSA requirements and to deal with any warranty questions or claims.

These are reasonable defaults. Some records may need to be kept for longer if the law requires it, and we will delete or anonymise information securely once we no longer need it.

7. Keeping it secure

We take sensible steps to protect your information from being lost, misused or accessed without permission. These include using secure, password-protected systems, limiting access to staff who need it, and choosing reputable service providers. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, but we do our best to protect anything you send us.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Erase your information in some circumstances (we may need to keep certain records, such as MOT records, by law).
  • Restrict how we use your information in some circumstances.
  • Object to us using your information where we rely on legitimate interests, and to any direct marketing.
  • Data portability: ask for information you gave us in a commonly used electronic format, where this applies.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on your consent.

There's no charge for making a request in most cases, and we'll respond within one month. We may need to check your identity first.

MOTs

Booking, results and what happens next.

How do I book an MOT?

You can book online on our MOT booking page: just choose a date and time that suits you and add your registration. If you prefer, give us a call on 01784 477153 and we’ll book you in.

When is my MOT due?

Your MOT due date is on your last MOT certificate. You can also check it for free on GOV.UK using your registration: check your MOT history. It also shows past results, mileage readings and any advisories.

You can have your MOT up to a month (minus a day) before it runs out and still keep the same renewal date.

Not sure? Just give us a call on 01784 477153 and we’ll check it for you.

How long does an MOT take?

The test itself usually takes around 30 minutes. We’ll let you know when you book if we need the car for longer.

What happens if my car fails?

Dean will talk you through exactly what failed and why. Once he’s gone through it with you, we’ll look to get you booked in for the repairs as soon as possible.

If a fault is classed as dangerous, the car can’t be driven until it’s fixed. We offer a free retest. Just get in touch with us first, as it may not need a full retest slot.

Are you an approved MOT testing station?

Yes. We’re a DVSA-approved MOT testing station, and our MOT tester Dean carries out the tests here at the garage.
